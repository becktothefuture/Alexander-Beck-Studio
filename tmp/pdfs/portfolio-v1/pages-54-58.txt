                 SIDE PROJECT
                                              ProKeyboard   0.4



                 As a side project, I’ve been creating a new kind
                 of mobile keyboard—one that better supports
                 focus, fluidity, and neurodiverse thinking. 

                 Drawing inspiration from Gboard and iOS, I’m
                 experimenting with subtle feedback loops, one-
                 handed modes, adaptive keyboard shapes and
                 AI-enhanced prediction—all while reimagining
                 how keyboard UI could evolve beyond its 2007
                 roots. 

                 It’s a passion project rooted in a belief: real
                 innovation often comes from the margins.
