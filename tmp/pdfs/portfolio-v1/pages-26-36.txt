                    anti-spoof hologram



                    I designed and prototyped a spoof-resistant
ideation




                    hologram feature—built using gyroscopic
                    inputs and dynamic layers—that could visually
                    authenticate a user’s age in-store without
                    requiring the vendor to scan a device. 

                    It combined a daily-changing pattern, symbolic
                    authentication, and motion interaction into a
                    screen-based artefact that store staff could
                    verify at a glance. Originally developed as an
                    R&D idea, the prototype was refined into a
                    production feature, and remains in use today.

                    verification for adult content sites, YOTI was

                    I was brought in to rescue a failing product: bad
