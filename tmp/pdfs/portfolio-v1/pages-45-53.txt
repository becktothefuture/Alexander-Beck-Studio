A bit about me
                    Rediscovering Code as a Creative Tool



                    I studied computer science for a year and
                    always wanted to bring that foundation back
                    into my design work. 

                    This project gave me that chance.
                    Understanding how systems work helped me
                    see where creative opportunities open up and
                    where structural constraints actually sit.

Experimentation
                    Built, Scrapped, Rebuilt



                    Most of the early experiments didn’t survive the
                    final cut—not because they weren’t exciting,
                    but because they didn’t serve the rhythm of the
                    experience. 

                    There were ideas that felt too heavy, too
                    ornamental, or too disconnected from the
                    spatial structure I was aiming for. What stayed
                    had to feel orchestrated. What left helped
                    define the edges.

AI assisted workflows
                        Generative as Iterative



                        I used AI tools throughout the process—not for
                        the final design, but to help imagine textures,
                        lighting, and environments.

                        I made a habit of jumping between diffusion
                        generations, and the live build, letting each
                        output inform the next. 

                        The process became a loop: generate,
                        interpret, refine, implement. It taught me that
                        when used with intention, generative design
                        can deepen the creative process rather than
                        dilute it.

Status quo
             Humbled by the Build


             This project forced me to get serious about
             performance. Early versions broke under the
             weight of canvas animations, ambient layers,
             and concurrent scripts—especially on mobile. 

             I learned to spot memory sinks, restructure
             scripts, and think of the browser as a limited     robot recording
             machine, not a blank canvas.

             The refactoring is still ongoing, but the system
             now runs leaner, cleaner, and with room to
             grow. The codebase has become a source of
             pride: documented, modular, and surprisingly
             elegant, given where it started.---
Demo
                 Entering the Cockpit



                 The final scene pulls you deeper into the
                 experience. You scroll into a spacecraft moving
                 through infinity—a visual metaphor for
                 exploration and focus. 

                 At its core is a cockpit, and inside it, a
                 responsive console: a computer with
                 switchable states, ambient feedback, and a
                 rhythm of its own. The portfolio lives here,
                 nested within this machine. 

                 It’s a world within a world, designed to feel
                 tactile, reactive, and alive. This moment
                 anchors the entire experience—something
                 quiet, cinematic, and deliberate.
