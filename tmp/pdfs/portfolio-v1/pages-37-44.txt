                    Reading the Signals



                    Analytics revealed behaviour that clashed with
ideation




                    assumptions. Users weren’t scrolling.
                    Promotions were getting missed. Drop-offs
                    spiked midway through booking. 

                    As a first step, it was crucial to reprioritise the
                    content structure, elevate offers, and place
                    booking functionality front and centre. It was
                    less about redesigning pages and more about
                    correcting how information showed up in
                    context.

                    Shaping Simplicity at Scale



                    Booking flows aren’t linear. Flexible dates,
ideation




                    extras, one-ways, returns—each adds a branch.
                    I designed a flow that revealed options as
                    needed rather than all at once. This reduced
                    decision fatigue and worked across mobile and
                    desktop without forked logic or redundant
                    layouts.
