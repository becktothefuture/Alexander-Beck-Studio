# Ink decisions

## Lab project illustrations

- Keep Lab placeholder art as standalone local SVG files under `react-app/app/public/assets/playground/images/`.
- Give every SVG a `viewBox`, `<title>`, and `<desc>`; do not embed raster or remote assets.
- Use simple geometric compositions and the established Lab palette so the set remains eclectic but coherent.
- Keep every work card axis-aligned. Motion and spatial placement must not rotate the work.
