# Depth Rings

- **ID:** `rift-rings`
- **Stage:** automation candidate
- **Route:** `/lab/rift-rings.html`

## Idea

Expansive concentric rings of site-colored circle bodies drift in alternating directions, shear sideways with mouse movement, and breathe inward or outward as the pointer moves vertically or a touch drag moves up and down.

## Why It Is Different

Kaleido Rift uses a mirrored polar lattice and petal-like shear. Rift Rings keeps the polar inspiration but removes the mirrored kaleidoscope structure: the composition is a larger field of independent concentric bands that extend far beyond the visible wall and feel like a spatial pressure system moving through the room.

## Material Fit

The simulation uses flat palette-driven circle bodies inside the existing wall surface. It avoids field lines, trails, glows, weather overlays, local lighting, or decorative scaffolding. The rings are pre-spaced by circumference and density so the visual reads as disciplined bands rather than overlapping circle piles.

## Interaction Model

Mouse movement and touch drag are mapped to one physical action: ring shear and spacing pressure. Horizontal movement pushes adjacent rings into opposing phase offsets. Vertical position expands or contracts the spacing, with stronger response while dragging on mobile. Idle motion keeps the rings drifting and breathing when no pointer input is active.

## Why Candidate Level

This should stay review-only until the expansive offscreen scale, body count, mobile performance, and pointer mapping are judged against the existing Daily Simulation set. It is intentionally wired as a lab route and not as a Daily Simulation entry.

## Promotion Criteria

Promote only if the default frame is immediately composed, the offscreen scale feels expansive rather than sparse, mobile drag is legible, reduced motion remains alive but calmer, and preview/browser checks show no visible overlap clutter or performance drop.

## Deletion Criteria

Delete it if it reads as a decorative orbit field, feels too close to Kaleido Rift, depends on density that causes muddy overlap, or cannot preserve a strong first frame on mobile without losing the expansive behavior.
