# Beach Ball Room

- Date: 2026-06-25
- Stage: collection
- Launch path: `/lab/beach-ball-room.html`

Beach Ball Room is a collection-only object-led simulation: a large circle-built beach ball moving through room-scale wall physics. The tuned default reduces the lab-like room-line treatment, slightly lowers the scale and rebound, and adds damping so the route reads as a finished review surface rather than a raw controls experiment.

The constraint fit remains catalog-driven: preview metadata is present, and live promotion is controlled by the `daily-rotation` stage in `react-app/app/src/data/simulationCatalog.json`. Beach Ball Room is currently outside daily rotation and outside the live narrative cycle.
