# Organising idea

## Previous hypothesis

A single restrained interaction can give a website a memorable identity more effectively than a feature-heavy experience.

## Current formulation

The project was not about reducing features for the sake of minimalism. It was about expressing McCann's renewed creative confidence through deliberate restraint. Every interaction, animation and piece of typography exists only to foreground the work itself. Simplicity becomes an opinionated design stance rather than a lack of design.

## Status

Refined

## Why

The interview consistently returned to one observation:

The challenge was never "how do we make a modern agency website?"

It became:

"How little interface can exist while still creating an unforgettable experience?"

That idea appears much stronger than the original hypothesis.

---

# Questions and raw answers

## 1. What was the surprising constraint at the beginning?

The client wanted almost nothing competing with the work.

The redesign followed McCann's new global identity, inspired by the "golden age" of advertising: big ideas, copywriting, confidence and clarity.

Rather than adding interaction patterns normally expected on agency websites, the team deliberately removed them.

The objective became allowing the work to stand completely on its own.

---

### Follow-up

How did that affect the interface?

Alexander described intentionally abandoning conventional UI ideas very early.

Examples included:

- expandable content
- complex navigation
- rich tagging systems
- decorative interface treatments
- unnecessary animation
- typical agency website patterns

Instead, typography became the interface.

Native browser behaviour became the interaction language.

Nothing existed purely for decoration.

---

## 2. How did you know the direction was successful?

There was no formal user testing.

Instead the measure was whether the website felt effortless.

Alexander repeatedly described the result as feeling:

- confident
- singular
- cinematic
- opinionated

The work itself became the interface.

Large typography.

Large imagery.

Large video.

Almost no surrounding chrome.

The absence of interface became part of McCann's identity.

Alexander explicitly described this as an opinionated creative stance rather than an evidence-based optimisation.

---

## 3. What was your personal contribution?

Alexander described his contribution across multiple layers.

### UX

Reduced the overall architecture to only a handful of primary destinations.

Focused the experience around browsing work rather than navigating pages.

Created an experience that feels almost like a slideshow combined with a lightbox.

---

### Interaction Design

Developed the interaction philosophy.

Navigation relies almost entirely on natural scrolling and gesture.

Avoided bespoke interaction wherever possible.

Wanted every interaction to "feel right" rather than feel designed.

---

### Motion

Created the motion language for the new McCann identity.

Designed how the McCann logo appears.

Integrated "Truth Well Told" into the opening experience as a central narrative device.

Expanded the static rebrand into motion and interaction.

---

### Visual Design

Pushed the interface toward an intentionally brutalist feeling.

No icons.

Almost no decorative interface.

Typography became the dominant visual language.

Created an experience that intentionally feels slightly old-school while remaining contemporary.

---

### Prototyping

Built the complete experience in Webflow.

Created a fully interactive prototype early.

Early prototyping became critical because the simplicity could only really be evaluated once experienced.

The prototype accelerated alignment with the client.

---

### Development

Implemented bespoke interaction and animation directly.

The Webflow prototype later became the basis for implementation in another CMS.

---

## 4. Team contribution

The direction was developed collaboratively.

Alexander worked closely with:

- his Creative Director
- the wider McCann leadership
- Daryl Lee (CEO)

Discussions repeatedly centred on one question:

"How much is too much?"

The project established a blueprint for how McCann should confidently present work under the new brand.

# Most valuable material

## Fascinating observation

The challenge was not simplifying the website.

The challenge was having enough confidence to remove almost everything.

The website deliberately rejects many interaction patterns that have become expected in modern portfolio sites.

---

## Design mechanism

The interface disappears behind:

- typography
- native gestures
- motion
- cinematic presentation
- extremely limited navigation

Rather than inventing interaction, the project carefully chose what *not* to design.

---

## Strongest anecdote

Building the experience in Webflow extremely early allowed everyone—including senior leadership—to experience the restraint instead of debating it abstractly.

The prototype demonstrated that simplicity could feel richer than feature-heavy alternatives.

---

- Established a clear presentation model for showcasing work.

These are Alexander's design interpretations rather than measured outcomes.
