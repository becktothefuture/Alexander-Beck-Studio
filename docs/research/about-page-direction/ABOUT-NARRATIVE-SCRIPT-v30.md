# About Me narrative script v30

I’m a designer and creative technologist. I work on products and systems whose behaviour is still being defined—especially when they need to earn trust as well as make sense.

Clarity can remove too much.

I notice what gets lost.

Speaking several languages made that hard to ignore: the same idea can feel direct in one and awkward in another. Communication Design let me explore those shifts through type, image and motion.

I have worked in agencies, startups and independently. At Denaline, I collaborated with developers on interfaces, icon systems and motion. That made me think about what screens did, not only how they looked. At Yoti, identity verification made product, brand and trust part of one problem.

I now consider product, brand and implementation together from the start.

I start with the people closest to the problem and test the brief’s assumptions. Then I make the thinking visible—a sketch, prototype, motion study or code—for the team to question.

I often set the creative direction, but I share work early and explain the choices. I want collaborators involved while their knowledge can still change what we make.

On a financial index-building project, complexity needed to be layered rather than hidden. Some interaction nuance was lost in implementation. It changed how closely I stay involved with the build.

[Project impressions]

Some questions become side projects. I am redesigning the iOS keyboard—not because anyone asked, but because I use it every day and remain unconvinced. I also keep returning to what AI and robotics mean for trust and privacy.

I want new technology to make sense.

I do not want it to feel anonymous.

I expect that tension to keep me busy.

Let’s talk.

If you’re shaping an unfamiliar product or service and still deciding how it should behave, I’d like to hear about it.
