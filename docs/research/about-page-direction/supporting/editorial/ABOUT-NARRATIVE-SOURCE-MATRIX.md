# About Me narrative source matrix

## Purpose

This matrix separates source material from editorial interpretation. It combines both interview rounds and maps the strongest material to the five-act framework. Nothing marked **VERIFY** should be treated as public-ready copy.

## Status key

- **SOURCE** — stated directly by Alexander in one of the answer documents.
- **VERIFY** — plausible and useful, but the public wording, contribution, date, outcome or permission needs confirmation.
- **EDITORIAL** — a proposed interpretation or narrative use, not a fact supplied verbatim.
- **OPTIONAL** — useful supporting proof or personality, but not required in the one-minute story.

## Narrative hierarchy

1. **Main idea:** Make complex things understandable without making them anonymous.
2. **Personal reason:** Language, technology and visual form trained a particular kind of attention.
3. **Professional expansion:** That attention grew from composition into behaviour, trust and systems.
4. **Present contribution:** Alexander finds organising ideas, makes them tangible and protects specificity through implementation.
5. **Future direction:** Continue working on consequential problems that are not yet neatly defined.
