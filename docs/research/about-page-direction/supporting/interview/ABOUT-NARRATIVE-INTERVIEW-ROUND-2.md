# About Me narrative questionnaire — Round 2

## Career, practice, skills and future

Round 1 established the central point of view: clarity should not remove character; complexity should be layered rather than denied; and digital work benefits from tactility, spatial memory, meaningful controls and a visible human footprint.

This round does not revisit that philosophy. It gathers the personal and professional evidence needed to make the About story credible, specific and recognisably yours.

## How to complete it

- Answer the 15 questions marked **CORE** first.
- Bullet points are welcome. Dates can be approximate, but mark anything uncertain **VERIFY**.
- Use project names, decisions and remembered moments rather than polished portfolio language.
- Separate what you personally did from what the wider team did.
- If a client, outcome or collaborator cannot be mentioned publicly, mark it **PRIVATE**.
- Preserve phrases you would actually say aloud.
