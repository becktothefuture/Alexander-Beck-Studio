# About Me storyboard v7

## Status

**Current storyboard direction.** This document maps `ABOUT-NARRATIVE-SCRIPT-v7.md` onto the existing About route. It is a content and visual-mapping brief, not an implementation change.

No new display copy is introduced here. Every displayed sentence below comes from V7; client context remains a logo block only.

## One continuous journey

**Fragmented idea → organising thought → curiosity across aesthetics and technology → six connected disciplines → complexity coheres → invitation to collaborate.**

The route keeps its existing eight sections, timing model and one-material point-field system. The cloud, calm field, discipline grid, living field and bust are consecutive states of the same points.

## Route mapping

### 01 — Intro

**Existing route section:** `promise` · spatial opener · `cluster-v1`

**Display copy**

> I help shape complex ideas into emotionally compelling experiences.

**Visual direction**

A distant, restrained point cloud sits ahead in the window. It is recognisably unresolved but not yet overwhelming: the first readable frame is calm, with enough density to promise the complexity ahead.

### 02 — Entering complexity

**Existing route section:** `complexity` · spatial centre · `cluster-v1` to `calm-field-v1`

**Display copy, in order**

> At the start, an idea can feel like everything at once.

> It has to be useful, original, technically possible and emotionally right, while its shape is still fragmented.

> This stage of an idea has always captivated me: learning what is there, understanding what matters to the people involved, and finding the organising thought that can bring it into focus.

**Visual direction**

The camera approaches and passes through the cloud as the first two thoughts appear. The points feel most numerous and least ordered while the fragmented shape is named. During the final thought, the cloud opens and resolves into the existing calm field. The change is caused by the organising thought: the material gains direction without becoming a different object.

### 03 — Editorial area one

**Existing route section:** `background` · editorial reading · continuing `calm-field-v1`

**Visible title**

> Perhaps that is why I have always been drawn to the space between aesthetics and technology.

**Body copy, in order**

> I studied Communication Design, then built my work across digital products, web design, identity, interfaces, motion, and creative uses of AI and technology.

> At the centre of it is curiosity: how things work, how they feel and how those qualities can strengthen one another.

> This is what draws me to emerging technology. I see the opportunity for it to play a useful role in people’s lives once it has been deconstructed, made tangible, made visual and made interactive enough to resonate.

> That curiosity has shaped my work with technology start-ups and creative agencies, on ambitious projects across identity, finance, mobility, healthcare and digital products.

> [Client logos]

**Visual direction**

The calm field remains broad and still enough to read. It is a pause after the cloud, not a second scene. Client context is a compact logo block inside the editorial flow: no client names, no additional caption and no new narrative claim.

### 04 — Practice reveal

**Existing route section:** `practice-reveal` · spatial constellation · `calm-field-v1` to `discipline-grid-v1`

**Display copy**

> Over time, it has grown into a way of working across six connected disciplines: Product Design, Experience Design, Art Direction, Motion & 3D, Creative Engineering and Parametric Systems.

**Visual direction**

The camera rises from the field to the existing grid. Six exact points become visible within it as the six disciplines are named. The grid is not an abstract backdrop; it is the visible structure of the way of working described in the sentence.

### 05 — Editorial area two

**Existing route section:** `disciplines` · editorial disciplines · continuing `discipline-grid-v1`

**Visible title**

> Each brings a different way of thinking to the work.

**Discipline list**

1. Product Design
2. Experience Design
3. Art Direction
4. Motion & 3D
5. Creative Engineering
6. Parametric Systems

**Body copy, in order**

> Product Design and Experience Design help me understand an opportunity and shape how an experience works. Art Direction gives it character and a point of view. Motion & 3D make behaviour, space and feeling tangible. Creative Engineering and Parametric Systems let me explore directly through code, tools and working systems.

> AI runs through all of this as a creative material and a practical capability, expanding what I can explore, generate and build.

> Moving between disciplines lets one kind of thinking affect another. An insight can reshape a product. A technical experiment can unlock a visual language. Motion can make an interaction easier to understand.

**Visual direction**

The six named editorial items correspond to the six emphasised points in the grid. As each item enters the reading area, its point becomes legible; it does not become a separate object. During the final paragraph, influence travels through the grid so the points begin behaving as one connected field.

### 06 — Bringing the idea to life

**Existing route section:** `bringing-life` · spatial living field · `discipline-grid-v1` to `living-field-v1`

**Display copy, in order**

> As those ways of thinking begin to influence one another, complexity starts to cohere.

> It takes on clear form, intuitive behaviour and a character people can trust.

> It becomes something they can relate to, interact with and feel.

> That is what excites me about bringing an idea to life.

**Visual direction**

The whole grid becomes the existing living field. Broad waves and colour movement spread across the complete material while the camera returns from the high grid view to a low forward flight. There are no added monoliths, objects, labels or world-building elements: the coherence comes from the whole field moving as one.

### 07 — Service proposition

**Existing route section:** `role` · editorial exit · continuing `living-field-v1`

**Display copy**

> That is the work I do with ambitious teams: bringing design, creative technology and AI together to give early ideas a form people can understand, trust and want to engage with.

**Visual direction**

The living field settles into its most coherent state behind the copy. This is the service statement and final editorial pause; it should not introduce a new visual system or another burst of movement.

The final text is the invitation and contact actions only: do not add an Alexander Beck name lock-up, profile line or secondary statement here.
