# About Me narrative script v7

I help shape complex ideas into emotionally compelling experiences.

At the start, an idea can feel like everything at once. It has to be useful, original, technically possible and emotionally right, while its shape is still fragmented.

This stage of an idea has always captivated me: learning what is there, understanding what matters to the people involved, and finding the organising thought that can bring it into focus.

Perhaps that is why I have always been drawn to the space between aesthetics and technology. I studied Communication Design, then built my work across digital products, web design, identity, interfaces, motion, and creative uses of AI and technology.

At the centre of it is curiosity: how things work, how they feel and how those qualities can strengthen one another.

This is what draws me to emerging technology. I see the opportunity for it to play a useful role in people’s lives once it has been deconstructed, made tangible, made visual and made interactive enough to resonate.

That curiosity has shaped my work with technology start-ups and creative agencies, on ambitious projects across identity, finance, mobility, healthcare and digital products.

[Client logos]

Over time, it has grown into a way of working across six connected disciplines: Product Design, Experience Design, Art Direction, Motion & 3D, Creative Engineering and Parametric Systems.

Each brings a different way of thinking to the work. Product Design and Experience Design help me understand an opportunity and shape how an experience works. Art Direction gives it character and a point of view. Motion & 3D make behaviour, space and feeling tangible. Creative Engineering and Parametric Systems let me explore directly through code, tools and working systems.

AI runs through all of this as a creative material and a practical capability, expanding what I can explore, generate and build. Moving between disciplines lets one kind of thinking affect another. An insight can reshape a product. A technical experiment can unlock a visual language. Motion can make an interaction easier to understand.

As those ways of thinking begin to influence one another, complexity starts to cohere. It takes on clear form, intuitive behaviour and a character people can trust. It becomes something they can relate to, interact with and feel.

That is what excites me about bringing an idea to life.

That is the work I do with ambitious teams: bringing design, creative technology and AI together to give early ideas a form people can understand, trust and want to engage with.
