## 04 - Editorial text

That has been the thread through my career so far. I studied Communication Design because I was interested in how ideas become clear: through language, form, sequence and the small decisions that change how something is understood.

Early agency roles gave me a grounding in print, cultural projects and visual systems. Moving into digital products widened that into interfaces, motion, behaviour and trust, where product, brand and system start to depend on one another.

Emerging technology interests me when it can become useful in daily life. It needs to be seen, tested and experienced, not simply explained.

My career has taken me through technology startups and creative agencies, across digital products, web design, identity, interfaces, motion and the creative use of AI and technology. The projects have spanned identity, finance, mobility, healthcare and digital products.

## 08 - Editorial text

Product Design and Experience Design help me understand the situation, the users and the behaviour an experience needs. Art Direction gives the idea a recognisable character. Motion & 3D make timing, space and response easier to feel. Creative Engineering and Parametric Systems let me test ideas directly through code, tools and working systems.

AI sits inside that mix as both a creative material and a practical capability. I use it to explore, generate, prototype and extend what can be made, while keeping judgement close to the decisions that matter.

The disciplines are most useful when they affect one another. A prototype can reveal the right visual language. A motion study can simplify an interaction. A line of copy can change how someone understands a feature.
