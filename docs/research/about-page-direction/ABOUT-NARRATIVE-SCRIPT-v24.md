# About Me narrative script v24

I help shape complexity into compelling experiences.

I’ve always been drawn to the complicated bit.

New technology. New behaviours. How to inspire trust.

The interesting part is where those things meet: when a powerful capability remains unfamiliar, its implications are unclear and a service must explain itself without overshadowing the experience.

I studied Communication Design because I wanted to understand how ideas become clear through words, aesthetics, hierarchy, motion, systems and the interplay between them. Language taught me that meaning shifts with what is said and how it is carried. Agency work sharpened my visual judgement, craft and pace. Product work showed me that behaviour, brand and trust often belong to the same problem.

[Client logos]

No single discipline resolves complexity on its own.

So I move between several.

Product Design

Experience Design

Art Direction

Motion & 3D

Creative Engineering

Parametric Systems

Close attention comes first. I identify the organising idea, then give it a form the team can examine, question, test and develop. That is where useful disagreement begins.

AI extends what I can explore and make. Its value depends on human judgement and recognising when the result has become generic.

When an idea begins to cohere…

…it finds rhythm, character and use…

…and something new emerges.

I work with ambitious teams while the problem is still taking shape—especially where design, technology and AI must inform the direction from the outset.

If you’re building something new and need it to make sense to people, let’s talk.
