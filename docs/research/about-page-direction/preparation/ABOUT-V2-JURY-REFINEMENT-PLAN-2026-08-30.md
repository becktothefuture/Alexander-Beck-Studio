# About Me — jury refinement plan

Date: 30 August 2026\
Status: **review complete; proposal ready for implementation, not implemented**\
Audience: prospective clients, hiring teams and collaborators meeting Alex through the About page.

**Follow-up direction:** the user's new requirement for a distinct moving culmination is resolved in the [culmination and travel implementation plan](ABOUT-V2-CULMINATION-AND-TRAVEL-PLAN-2026-08-30.md). It supersedes this document's corridor-only ending, no-new-terminal-geometry restriction, fixed gap/camera assumptions, proposed lower mobile invitation position and build order. The personal copy edit and retained career/discipline/client evidence still apply. Read the follow-up before implementation; neither proposal is implemented.

## Decision

Keep the world. Make the person clearer, the edit shorter and the ending complete.

The current page has a recognisable visual language, useful career evidence and an effective final composition. Its main weakness is uneven emphasis: the person arrives behind a generic page title, several ideas repeat, the method has a narrow reading window, and the invitation can look finished before its actions appear. One local landscape transition still produces touching colour.

The next pass should be a bounded refinement, not another scene rebuild. No new biography section, showreel, portrait card, logo carousel, soundtrack, geometry or particle climax is needed.

## Review basis and limits

Three independent agents reviewed the page through **simulated** Story/Creative Idea, Design Craft and Film/Digital Craft perspectives. They are not real Cannes Lions jurors. No award result, conversion improvement or visitor-research result is claimed.

The lenses use the published emphasis on idea and execution in [Design Lions](https://www.canneslions.com/awards/lions/design), aesthetic and interaction craft in [Digital Craft Lions](https://www.canneslions.com/awards/lions/digital-craft), and direction, cinematography and editing in [Film Craft Lions](https://www.canneslions.com/awards/lions/film-craft). All three official pages were opened. There is no basis for a results score, so none is supplied.

The personal-origin/work-history principle was checked against the current [Daniel Sun About page](https://danielsun.space/about). Borrow its direct introduction and narrative progression, not its biography, phrasing or layout.

### Method: one title, three concise paragraphs

> I set a clear direction and invite collaborators to challenge it while decisions are open. I stay involved through delivery, keeping the central idea visible as the details evolve.

This keeps questioning, making, collaboration, direction and delivery. It does not turn the method into a generic innovation claim.

Retire `text-life-form`; do not leave its old slot empty. Keep `text-life-momentum` as the full title. Start its combined minimum at **0.95 screens as a test value**, then measure actual wrapping. The story juror suggested merging the method; the motion juror preferred merging the thesis. This plan chooses the method: five words do not need two independent dramatic resets. Keep the personal thesis pair and, especially, the user's requested closing pair.

## 2. Timing, duration and travel

### P1: shorten only the late empty passage

Current final sheets contain a **2.40 story-WU gap** after the method and before the first closing title. It occupies about 9% of the route while repeating the split-bank composition. One WU is tied to a scrollport-height unit; it is not a fixed duration in seconds.

Give this use a dedicated **`arrival` gap of 0.80 WU**. Do not change the shared `passage` preset: that also contains real portal/gate travel. Retain both closing titles, their readable spans and the authored final camera.

| Isolated gap-only experiment | Mobile | Desktop |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Current measured story length | 26.452069 WU | 27.152033 WU |
| With 2.40 → 0.80 gap only | 24.852069 WU | 25.552033 WU |
| First closing title starts | 21.732069 WU | 22.312033 WU |
| Camera locks | 23.232069 WU | 23.832033 WU |

The main reviewer independently reproduced valid/certifiable cue order and monotone progress across 10,001 samples per profile; preceding anchors remain unchanged. This does **not** certify the proposed motion visually. The shorter interval raises peak arrival progress per WU by about 1.7× in the motion review's calculation. Reject a visible speed hump or late snap in the rendered candidate.

The full proposal's duration is **not yet measured**: changed words, line breaks, block floors and the merged title all affect it. Do not subtract word percentages from scroll length. Recompile after native fonts settle. The result must be shorter than the current route without reducing the preserved reading intervals. Do not promise a fixed one-minute experience: the visitor controls reading and scrolling.

At a deliberate test rate of 0.5 WU/s, this gap is 4.8 seconds now and 1.6 seconds proposed. At 1.0 WU/s it is 2.4 and 0.8 seconds. These are repeatable QA rates, not predictions of visitor behaviour.

### P2: test the terrain-to-gate progression join

Source sampling gives about 209.94 authored camera-path units over 0.46 story-WU at the landscape-release/gate-entry join, much faster per scroll distance than the preceding terrain span. These are not metres or frame-rate measurements.

Fresh small-step captures show the broad terrain retreating to the frame edge, a small distant gate appearing, then a rapid change in gate scale. That warrants a progression-envelope test. It does not prove missing geometry or a bad Blender camera, and settled captures alone cannot certify continuous acceleration.

Test a bounded continuous slope at the existing semantic join. Preserve the current path, FOV, orientation samples and **1.10-WU actual gate passage**. Do not add more looping or a global smoothing delay. Compare forward and reverse passes at 0.5 and 1.0 WU/s plus a fast gesture. If smoothing merely makes the bridge feel empty for longer, reject it and retain current travel. Keep this work conditional on a visible improvement, not a mathematical preference.

### P1: the invitation must finish when the visitor stops

Keep the exact sequence:

> If you’re shaping something…\
> …that needs more than one way of thinking…\
> Let’s begin.

Keep the existing supporting sentence, email-copy action and LinkedIn action.

**Verified defect:** the final title can be fully visible while the actions are hidden and inert. Main reproduced this after a multi-second stop on desktop and mobile. The source makes actions depend on 62–80% scroll progress through the invitation. Waiting cannot complete them.

Add an About-local arrival state that starts once the invitation is entered and the camera is locked. Let title → rule → supporting copy → actions finish on elapsed time without another wheel movement. Start with a 900ms completion target; acceptance is all controls visible and usable within **1.2 seconds**, including any shared title pause. Reduced motion resolves immediately. Retain the shared typography/entrance language rather than changing every route's bookend.

Define cancellation and re-entry explicitly: reverse past the invitation resets its arrival and removes invisible actions from focus; a new entry starts cleanly; a fast jump to the end reveals a usable endpoint. If the action being hidden owns focus, return focus to the About scrollport with `preventScroll`; do not steal unrelated focus. Route teardown cancels the local sequence, and changing to reduced motion resolves it immediately. No stale timers, duplicate loops or React state updates on every animation frame.

For mobile only, test lowering the entire final title-to-actions group modestly within the clear aisle. Keep a short-portrait safeguard. Do not force a 50% anchor, split the group, move the camera or resize buttons to make it fit.

Normal motion must keep the point material moving indefinitely around a fixed final camera. Reduced motion must keep the scene calm. Do not use camera drift to manufacture perpetual movement.

## 5. Observable acceptance gates

1. **Person and voice:** Alex is named in the settled opener; the early title pair supplies a real biographical fact. No new unsupported claims. The voice remains direct, first-person and practical.
2. **Copy and structure:** exactly the approved five career rows, six discipline descriptions, 15 clients and two closing titles. No empty retired title. Word count at or below the approved 419-word candidate, within all hard ceilings.
3. **Reading:** native 320/390 and desktop full-opacity reading positions; every career row and editorial line reachable forward/reverse. At each method paragraph's nominated focus stop, its leading complete thought is readable at full strength. The first short paragraph fits completely where the clear space permits. Verify forward and reverse without seeking an unusually favourable offset; record the focus positions before capture. No font-size reduction as a workaround.
4. **Composition:** inspect opener entry, a ten-second hold and exit; inspect all client rows; inspect mobile final-group bounds. No persistent dot crossing of title/body/action labels and no shell movement.
5. **Travel and length:** measured total shorter than baseline; no new long scenery-only pause. Preserve portal/gate landmarks and 1.10-WU gate traversal. Test constant-rate, small-step, fast and reverse travel. Valid cue math is necessary but not sufficient.
6. **Contact arrival:** stop at 10%, 25% and 50% of the invitation for two seconds. Both actions become visible and usable without more scroll. Repeat after fast entry, reversal and re-entry. Check Tab/Shift+Tab and Enter without invisible focus targets; verify the copy interaction without sending messages or opening an external profile unnecessarily.
7. **End motion:** at 1/3/6/15-second holds the normal scene keeps moving with a fixed camera. Reduced motion remains static at the scene layer. Use scene-level evidence, not whole-page pixel equality: the paper grain can change independently.
8. **Material identity:** closely sample terrain release and both passage joins; no touching same-colour ribbons, missing forms, popping points or thin helper rings. Stable counts/buffer identities supplement visual evidence; they cannot prove geometry remains readable.
9. **Coverage:** Chromium and WebKit serial runs; supported desktop/mobile widths, 320px, short portrait, light/dark, representative high-contrast palettes, DPR 1/2/3, normal/reduced motion, scroll restoration and SPA return. Preserve the intentional unsupported-short-landscape guard. A real-phone performance check remains a separate requirement; do not claim 60 FPS from screenshots.
10. **Handoff:** regenerate at least the current 75-frame-per-main-profile contact density plus fine transition samples and held endings. Open every page, record remaining exceptions and compare to the frozen baseline. Build/test success is not visual approval.
