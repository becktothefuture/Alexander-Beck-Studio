# About Me narrative script v26

I’m a multidisciplinary designer and creative technologist. I shape complex ideas into products, systems and experiences people can understand, trust and remember.

Make it understandable.

Keep it worth remembering.

Speaking several languages taught me that meaning changes with context. Communication Design turned that observation into a practice spanning words, images, structure and motion.

My work has moved between agencies, startups and independent projects. At Denaline, working with developers took me from visual design into interfaces, icon systems and motion. At Yoti, identity verification made product behaviour, brand and trust part of the same problem.

The scope now includes UX, creative direction, design systems, front-end prototyping, 3D and AI. I use whichever discipline helps the whole experience cohere.

I begin with the people closest to the problem and the assumptions inside the brief. Then I make the thinking tangible—a sketch, prototype, motion study or code—so the team has something real to question and improve.

I often set the creative direction, but I put ideas in front of people early and explain the reasoning. I want collaborators involved while the interesting decisions are still open.

A financial index-building project taught me why implementation matters. The interface layered complexity instead of hiding it; when interaction nuance was lost in the build, I began staying closer to delivery.

Some questions become side projects. I use AI to explore and prototype, but the judgement remains mine. I am also redesigning the iOS keyboard in my spare time—not because anyone asked, but because I use it every day and remain unconvinced.

AI. Trust. Privacy. Robotics.

The important questions are still open.
