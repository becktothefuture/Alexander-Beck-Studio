## 04 - Editorial text

The real questions usually sit there: what people need to understand, what they need to trust, and how a new capability becomes something they can use.

I studied Communication Design to understand how ideas become clear: through words, images, hierarchy, motion, systems and the small decisions between them. Agency work gave me visual culture, craft and pace. Product work brought the questions closer to use.

Emerging technology keeps raising those questions in new ways. Powerful capabilities need shape, language, behaviour and a reason to be there. My experience has moved through startups and creative agencies across digital products, web design, identity, interfaces, motion and creative technology, with projects in finance, mobility, healthcare, digital identity and product innovation.

## 08 - Editorial text

Product and Experience Design help me understand who it is for, what needs to happen and where an experience can become simpler. Art Direction gives the idea character. Motion & 3D make timing, space and behaviour tangible. Creative Engineering and Parametric Systems let me test ideas as prototypes, tools and systems.

AI runs through this as material and capability: a way to explore more directions, build faster and get to working possibilities sooner. The important part is still judgement.

Moving between disciplines is where things unlock. A product insight can change the visual language. A technical experiment can suggest a new interaction. A sentence can carry the experience when it lands at the right moment.

The point is to give an idea structure, clarity and character: usable enough to test, clear enough to trust, specific enough to feel real.

I work with ambitious teams who need design, creative technology and AI to shape direction early and carry it into convincing detail.
