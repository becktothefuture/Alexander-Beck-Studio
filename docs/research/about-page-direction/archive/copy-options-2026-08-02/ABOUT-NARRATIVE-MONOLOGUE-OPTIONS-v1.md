## Comparison notes

- Version A is the clearest baseline. Its strongest quality is ease and warmth.
- Version B carries the most emotional nuance. Its strongest quality is its concern for how complexity feels to people.
- Version C creates the strongest forward momentum. Its strongest quality is the link between human judgement, technology, and making something new.
- All three preserve the same topic order and can be loaded into the existing runtime without changing the animation structure.
- The next review should select passages and tone, not combine every preferred sentence. A hybrid must still be rewritten and recounted as one monologue.
