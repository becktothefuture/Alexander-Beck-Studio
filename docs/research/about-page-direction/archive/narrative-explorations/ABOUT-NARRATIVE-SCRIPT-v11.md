## 04 — Editorial text

I first encountered it through language: how words, visual form and systems shape what an idea means in use. I studied Communication Design to understand how composition, form and shape affect meaning. Digital work took that interest into interfaces, motion, behaviour and trust, where product, brand and system stopped being separate concerns.

That is why emerging technology interests me when it can become genuinely useful in people’s lives. It needs to be seen, tested and experienced—not simply explained.

Since then, I have worked with technology start-ups and creative agencies across digital products, web design, identity, interfaces, motion and the creative use of AI and technology. The work has included ambitious projects in identity, finance, mobility, healthcare and digital products.

## 08 — Editorial text

Product and Experience Design are how I get close to the work: learning what matters, understanding the context and shaping how an experience works. Art Direction gives the work a point of view. Motion & 3D make behaviour, space and feeling tangible. Creative Engineering and Parametric Systems let me test ideas through code, tools and working systems.

AI runs through this as a creative material and a practical capability.

I use these disciplines together. A technical experiment can reshape the visual language; motion can make an interaction easier to understand; a clearer piece of copy can change how people understand a feature.
