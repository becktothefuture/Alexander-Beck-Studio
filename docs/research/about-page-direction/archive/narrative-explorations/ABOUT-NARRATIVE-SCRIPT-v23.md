# About Me narrative script v23

I help shape complexity into compelling experiences.

I’ve always been drawn to the complicated bit.

New technology. New behaviours. How to inspire trust.

The interesting part is where those things meet: when a powerful capability is unfamiliar and a service has to explain what it changes without getting in the way.

I studied Communication Design because I wanted to understand how ideas become clear through words, aesthetics, hierarchy, motion, systems and the interplay between them. Agency work gave me visual culture, craft and pace. Product work showed me that behaviour, brand and trust often belong to the same problem.

[Client logos]

Resolving complexity rarely sits within one discipline.

So I move between several.

Product Design

Experience Design

Art Direction

Motion & 3D

Creative Engineering

Parametric Systems

I go deep into the problem, find the organising idea and make it tangible enough for a team to question, test and build.

AI helps me explore and build faster, but its value depends on direction, human judgement and knowing when the work feels generic.

When ideas become intentional…

…they find rhythm, character and use…

…and something new emerges.

I work with ambitious teams on problems that are not neatly defined yet—especially where design, technology and AI need to shape direction together.

If you’re building something new and need it to make sense to people, let’s talk.
