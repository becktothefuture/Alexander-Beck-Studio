## 04 — Editorial text

I first became interested in it through language: how words, visual form and systems shape what an idea means in use. I studied Communication Design to understand how composition, form and shape affect meaning.

Early agency roles began with print and cultural projects. Moving into digital products brought me to interfaces, motion, behaviour and trust—where product, brand and system stopped being separate concerns.

Emerging technology interests me when it can become genuinely useful in people’s lives. It needs to be seen, tested and experienced—not simply explained.

My career has taken me through technology start-ups and creative agencies, across digital products, web design, identity, interfaces, motion, and the creative use of AI and technology. The projects have spanned identity, finance, mobility, healthcare and digital products.

## 08 — Editorial text

I use Product and Experience Design to understand what matters, make sense of the context and shape how an experience works. Art Direction establishes a point of view. Motion & 3D make behaviour, space and feeling tangible. Creative Engineering and Parametric Systems let me test ideas through code, tools and working systems.

AI is both a creative material and a practical capability within that mix.

Bringing these disciplines together changes the outcome. A technical experiment can reshape the visual language; motion can make an interaction easier to understand; a clearer piece of copy can change how someone understands a feature.
