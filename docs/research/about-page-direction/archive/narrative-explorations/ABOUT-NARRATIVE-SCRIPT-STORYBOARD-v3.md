# About Me narrative script and modular storyboard v3

## Status

**Working text and framework for manual refinement.** This version steps back from the fixed V2 sequence. It separates the story from a modular set of text-free visual frames so the narrative and imagery can be rearranged independently.

Client names, career details, professional positioning and availability language remain subject to final public verification.

## Current objective

Create a conversational About story that lets someone meet Alexander, understand what he believes in, see the breadth of his multidisciplinary practice and recognise how creativity, technology and AI come together to shape something new.

The page should feel informative, useful and human. The visual journey adds emotion and makes the multidisciplinary thinking tangible; it does not pretend the working process is unique.

## Revised framework

**Promise → unfinished ideas → curiosity and background → clients and interests → six disciplines → multidisciplinary synthesis → immersive outcome → professional profile**

### Movement 1 — Enter the idea

A very short promise introduces the service. Loose thoughts appear in space as the camera approaches and enters the dense cloud. These are fragments rather than explanatory copy.

### Editorial area 1 — Meet Alexander

The cloud opens into calm. A short editorial section introduces the curiosity connecting creativity and technology, followed by a small list of recurring interests and a concise client line.

### Movement 2 — The practice expands

The profile leads naturally into the six disciplines. The field becomes a precise grid and the six areas of expertise appear within it.

### Editorial area 2 — How the disciplines connect

The reader scrolls down through the grid. Short prose passages explain what each discipline contributes, how AI runs through the practice and how moving between them creates room for innovation.

### Movement 3 — Experience the outcome

The grid begins responding as a whole. The camera travels through an immersive environment that feels exciting, simple, purposeful, distinctive and capable of becoming genuinely new.

### Epilogue — Profile and contact

The field settles into the bust. The final copy states the professional profile and working territory plainly.

## Complete working script

### Promise

> I help shape complex ideas into emotionally compelling experiences.

### Words inside the approach

These appear separately in space as the camera approaches and enters the cloud:

> Fragments of ideas.

> More than a few loose ends.

### Editorial area 1 — Curiosity is the common thread

> I have always been curious about how things work and how they make people feel. That curiosity took me from Computer Science to Communication Design, then into products, brands, interfaces, motion and creative technology. I have spent most of my career moving between these worlds, learning enough of each to notice where the interesting connections are.

**Things I keep coming back to**

- New technology with a human purpose.
- Clear ideas with a strong visual point of view.
- Products and systems with room for character.
- Making the thing, because a deck can only do so much.

> Along the way, I have worked with Yoti, S&P Global, Bentley, SunExpress, McCann and the American Heart Association across identity, finance, mobility, healthcare and digital products.

### Transition into the grid

> Over time, that curiosity has become a multidisciplinary practice.

### Disciplines

- Product Design
- Experience Design
- Art Direction
- Motion & 3D
- Creative Engineering
- Parametric Systems

### Editorial area 2 — How the disciplines connect

> Product Design and Experience Design help define what we are making and how it should work. Art Direction gives it an identity and a point of view. Motion & 3D make behaviour, space and emotion tangible. Creative Engineering and Parametric Systems turn ideas into working experiments and adaptable systems.
>
> AI now runs through much of this practice too. I use it to explore, prototype and build faster, and to open directions that would have been impractical only a few years ago. Judgement gives those possibilities direction. The disciplines give them form.
>
> The disciplines overlap as the idea develops. One opens a question. Another helps answer it. Together they give me more ways to explore what something could become and more ways to make it real.
>
> That is where innovation happens for me: in the movement between ideas, tools and forms. Sometimes the result is a product. Sometimes it is a system, an interaction, a world or a new way of working. The useful part is finding the form that makes the idea click.

## Image contract for the modular kit

- Every frame is a separate landscape PNG with no narrative text, labels, UI copy or watermark.
- Use the same flat, solid circular-dot material throughout.
- Preserve the site's neutral field with controlled green, cobalt, orange and acid yellow-green accents.
- Use density, position, scale and displacement for depth.
- Avoid lines, rings, wireframes, glow, fog, shaded spheres and generic science-fiction scenery.
- The six disciplines are only represented by six highlighted dots and shared colour behaviours. They never become literal objects or separate worlds.
- New immersive frames should feel designed and purposeful rather than decorative or chaotic.
- The bust should occupy roughly half the image height and remain the quietest final state.
