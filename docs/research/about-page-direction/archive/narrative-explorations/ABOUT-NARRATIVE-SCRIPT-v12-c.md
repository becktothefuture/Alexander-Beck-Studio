## 04 - Editorial text

That is the territory I have built my career around.

I studied Communication Design because I wanted to understand how meaning is made: in words, images, systems, motion and the gaps between them. Agency work gave me a grounding in visual culture and craft. Product work moved the questions closer to behaviour: how a service explains itself, how a screen earns trust, how a system holds together under pressure.

This is why emerging technology keeps my attention. New tools become useful only when they are translated into something people can see, try and understand.

My experience has taken me through startups and creative agencies, across digital products, web design, identity, interfaces, motion and creative technology, with projects in finance, mobility, healthcare, identity and product innovation.

## 08 - Editorial text

Product and Experience Design give me the shape of the problem: users, needs, behaviour and structure. Art Direction gives it a point of view. Motion & 3D add timing, space and feel. Creative Engineering and Parametric Systems turn the idea into something that can be tested, changed and made real.

AI belongs here as a working material. It helps me explore options, build tools around the task and push a direction further without handing over judgement.

The point is not breadth for its own sake. It is creating enough contact between strategy, design and implementation that the idea does not flatten as it moves through the project.
