# About Me narrative script v5

I help shape complex ideas into emotionally compelling experiences.

At the beginning, an idea can feel like everything at once. It has to be useful, original, technically possible and emotionally right—all before its shape is clear.

I enjoy getting close at that point: learning the subject, understanding what matters to the people involved and finding a direction that can hold it together.

That instinct has been the thread through my own work. I have always had one foot in the technical and one in the visual. I studied Computer Science before Communication Design, then built a practice across digital products, identity, interfaces, motion and creative technology.

Curiosity connects it all: how something works, how it feels and how those qualities can strengthen one another. It keeps drawing me back to emerging technology with a useful role in people’s lives, products and systems with character, and visual ideas that work as well as they look. It is also why I like making ideas tangible early. A deck can only do so much.

That curiosity has shaped work with Yoti, S&P Global, Bentley, SunExpress, McCann and the American Heart Association, across identity, finance, mobility, healthcare and digital products.

Working across those contexts has built a practice around six connected disciplines: Product Design, Experience Design, Art Direction, Motion & 3D, Creative Engineering and Parametric Systems.

Product Design and Experience Design help me understand an opportunity and shape how an experience works. Art Direction gives an idea character and a point of view. Motion & 3D make behaviour, space and feeling tangible. Creative Engineering and Parametric Systems let me explore directly through code, tools and working systems.

AI sits within that practice as both a creative material and a practical capability, expanding what I can explore, generate and build. Judgement, craft and context give those possibilities purpose.

The value comes from the exchange between disciplines. An insight can reshape a product. A technical experiment can unlock a visual language. Motion can make an interaction easier to understand. Each move changes the next.

As those ways of thinking begin to influence one another, an idea gathers momentum. Something that was difficult to picture begins to take form, behaviour and character. It becomes something people can understand, interact with and feel.

This is how an idea comes to life.

This is the role I play for ambitious teams: bringing design, creative technology and AI together to shape new products, services and experiences—from early direction through to the details that make them convincing.

Alexander Beck.
