# About Me: two new narrative approaches

## Status

Working narrative directions for review. Both use the active framework in `ABOUT-NARRATIVE-FRAMEWORK.md`, the preserved questionnaire answers and the point-cloud transformation as one continuous story.

These are not public-ready scripts. Project details marked **VERIFY** require contribution, context and outcome approval before publication.

## Shared presentation rules

- No visible eyebrow labels, section numbers or noun headings in the experience.
- One large narrative text role carries spatial sentences, highlighted sentences and the main vertical prose.
- The approximately 18px text role is reserved for factual details and descriptions inside vertical sections.
- Highlighted sentences stay inside the prose flow instead of becoming conventional titles.
- The point cloud progresses through density, signal, structure, stillness, response and possibility.
- Client and career context appears as supporting evidence, never as a résumé ledger.

## Approach A — What must survive

### Core idea

Clarity is a judgement about what must survive. The story begins with the cost of reduction, then shows how attention, structure and prototyping can make complexity usable without removing character.

This is the closest evolution of the original **clarity without flattening** research direction. It is reflective, materially grounded and less tied to a particular technology moment.

### Reader journey

**loss → attention → structure → false resolution → life → invitation**

### Working narrative

#### Spatial opening — density

> The hardest part of making something clear is deciding what not to lose.

The reader moves into a dense field. The sentence becomes readable only at one stable plane; the cloud remains rich rather than resolving immediately.

#### Vertical prose — attention

> I came to design through language and technology—two systems for carrying an idea from one mind to another. Visual form taught me that attention is shaped by choices: what comes forward, what recedes, what connects and what disappears.
>
> **Clarity is not the absence of complexity. It is a decision about what must survive.**

**Detail prose (~18px):** Computer Science led into Communication Design in Mainz. The work later expanded from composition and visual language into interfaces, behaviour, trust, products and systems. **VERIFY exact education wording.**

The field recedes while small local relationships become visible. The highlighted sentence sits inside the passage rather than above it as a title.

#### Spatial turn — signal becoming structure

> Complexity is not the enemy. Confusion is.

Local signals hold. The point cloud begins to resolve into a navigable structure while retaining depth and variation.

#### Vertical prose — practice

> The work often begins before the format is known. I immerse myself in the problem, look for the organising idea, and make it tangible enough for people to test together. Prototypes turn opinions into something we can experience. Systems help the idea travel. Creative direction protects the things that made it worth pursuing in the first place.

**Detail prose (~18px):** Possible proof, pending contribution wording: layered financial configuration for S&P Global; movement as part of a trust interaction at Yoti; tactility inside a usable system for the American Heart Association. **VERIFY all project language.**

The main passage remains large. Evidence appears quietly beside or after the relevant sentence and can be skipped without breaking the argument.

#### Spatial hinge — perfect order

> A perfect system can still feel lifeless.

The point field reaches complete order and stops. The pause is allowed to become slightly uncomfortable.

#### Vertical prose into response

> Character is not decoration added after the useful work. It lives in how something responds, how movement guides attention, how a constraint becomes a useful choice, and how a person can sense that somebody cared.
>
> **The aim is not less complexity. It is more meaning, made legible.**

One point responds, then the system develops a restrained pulse and wave. Structure remains visible beneath the change.

#### Spatial close — possibility

> I want to keep working where technology, trust and human behaviour are still being figured out.

**Vertical exit detail (~18px):** If you are building something difficult, consequential or genuinely new, get in touch. Email is primary; LinkedIn is secondary.

The field opens and settles without becoming inert. The small contact line appears only after the spatial sentence has resolved into a short vertical exit.

### Strengths

- Most faithful to the research and to Alexander's strongest questionnaire language.
- Gives the point cloud a complete dramatic role rather than isolated visual states.
- Makes biography personal without turning it into chronology.
- Can remain relevant even as tools and technology change.

### Risk

The clarity/complexity territory can become familiar design language if the practice passage remains abstract. The final version needs at least one verified, observable project or prototype detail.

## Approach B — Copies of copies

### Core idea

Patterns make work possible at scale, but patterns repeated without judgement can replace the premise. The story asks how systems can remain learnable and reliable while still feeling specific to the people and problem they serve.

This approach draws more directly from Alexander's observation about AI-generated best practice producing “copies of copies.” It is sharper, more contemporary and more provocative than Approach A.

### Reader journey

**convergence → recognition → intention → divergence → living system → unresolved future**

### Working narrative

#### Spatial opening — convergence

> Everything is becoming easier to generate. And harder to remember.

Thousands of points enter the same repeated behaviour. The field is polished, coherent and increasingly anonymous.

#### Vertical prose — the usefulness and danger of patterns

> Patterns are useful. They make systems consistent, products learnable and work possible at scale. But when the same answers are repeated without judgement, the pattern starts to replace the premise. We get copies of copies: polished, usable and strangely anonymous.
>
> **The problem is not sameness. It is sameness without intention.**

**Detail prose (~18px):** Language, technology and visual form were the first systems Alexander learned to read. Each has rules; each also depends on tone, context and choice.

A few points resist perfect convergence. Their difference reveals relationships that repetition had hidden.

#### Spatial turn — intention

> A system should create possibilities, not erase them.

The field organises into a stable plane, but several regions retain distinct density, rhythm and behaviour.

#### Vertical prose — a systematic but non-formulaic practice

> My work is systematic, but it is not formulaic. I look for the structure that can hold a difficult idea, then test where it should bend: through a prototype, a motion study, a new interaction or a deliberately small set of controls. AI expands the field of possible answers. It makes judgement more important, not less.
>
> **The best answer is sometimes a new interaction, not a better arrangement of familiar parts.**

**Detail prose (~18px):** Supporting proof may include the Yoti trust interaction, the S&P Global configuration model, the restrained McCann website interaction and the unfinished iOS keyboard exploration. **VERIFY project contribution language.**

#### Spatial hinge — difference becomes consequence

> One difference can change the whole field.

One restrained variation propagates through the ordered plane. The system gains movement and depth without losing its underlying rules.

#### Vertical prose — specificity

> The goal is not novelty everywhere. It is specificity where it matters: a response that makes an action understandable, a visual decision that belongs to this organisation, a moment of tactility that tells somebody the experience was made with care.
>
> **Reliability and character are not competing outcomes.**

The wave settles into several related behaviours rather than one uniform animation.

#### Spatial close — the unsettled pattern

> Some problems deserve more than the best-known answer.

**Vertical exit detail (~18px):** The next territory includes AI, trust, privacy, robotics, digital identity and other consequential questions whose patterns have not settled. Contact by email.

The ordered field opens into multiple possible paths rather than one final composition. The small factual line appears only in the vertical exit after the spatial close.

### Strengths

- Uses Alexander's most distinctive observation about contemporary design culture.
- Gives AI a clear philosophical role without turning the page into an AI capability pitch.
- Maps naturally to repeated point-cloud behaviour, divergence and propagation.
- Explains systematic thinking and character as complementary rather than opposing modes.

### Risk

Leading with generation and convergence may date faster or make the page sound like a critique of the industry. The personal lens must arrive early enough that the approach remains Alexander's story rather than a general essay about AI.

---

## Recommendation

Develop **Approach A — What must survive** as the primary narrative. It has the deeper emotional arc, the strongest continuity with the research and the clearest long-term relationship to the point-cloud transformation.

Keep **Approach B — Copies of copies** as a genuine alternative, not a collection of spare lines. If the two are later combined, borrow only one idea from B—the danger of pattern replacing premise—and place it as supporting evidence inside A. Do not merge both openings or both conceptual hinges.
