## Recommended complete story

### Opening

> The hardest part of making something clear is deciding what not to lose.

### Personal lens

I came to design through language and technology. Learning how composition, form and shape change what an idea communicates taught me to look before making—to notice hierarchy, rhythm and the small choices that give something its character. That attention still underpins my work.

### Expansion

> What began with visual form expanded into behaviour, trust and systems. The screen became one part of a much larger experience.

### Present practice

Today I work across experience design, systems, art direction and prototyping. I tend to be most useful when the problem is still ambiguous: immersing myself in the subject, finding an organising idea, and making it tangible enough for teams to question and improve together.

That has meant layering financial complexity for S&P Global and exploring how movement can communicate trust at Yoti. I use motion, code, 3D and AI when they help an idea become clearer or more convincing—not because every project needs them. The aim is work that feels specific to the problem and remains useful after the presentation is over.

### Invitation

> I want to keep working where technology, trust and human behaviour are still being figured out. If you are building something difficult, consequential or genuinely new, let’s talk.

## Expandable proof copy

These labels are supporting material, not part of the 209-word reading path.

### S&P Global — Layering complexity, not hiding it

An index-building interface had to support deep financial configuration without presenting every decision at once. The design organised complexity into layers while keeping advanced control available.

### Yoti — Movement as a signal of trust

An anti-spoof hologram connected physical movement with digital identity verification. The interaction made behaviour, brand and trust part of the same product moment.

### American Heart Association — A system with room for tactility

The work explored how softness, tactility and visual expression could live inside a coherent system that designers and developers could apply consistently.

### McCann — Restraint that remains memorable

A deliberately restrained website used a small interaction idea to create identity without turning the experience into spectacle.
