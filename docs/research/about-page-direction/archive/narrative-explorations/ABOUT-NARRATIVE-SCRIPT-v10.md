## 04 — Editorial text

Language has always interested me: how ideas move through words, visual form and the systems people use. I studied Communication Design to understand how composition, form and shape affect meaning. Digital work then took me into interfaces, motion, behaviour and trust, where product, brand and system stopped being separate concerns.

Emerging technology holds my attention when it can become genuinely useful in people’s lives. It needs to be seen, tested and experienced—not simply explained.

That has led to work with technology start-ups and creative agencies, across digital products, web design, identity, interfaces, motion, and the creative use of AI and technology. It has included ambitious projects in identity, finance, mobility, healthcare and digital products.

## 08 — Editorial text

Product and Experience Design are how I get close to the work: learning what matters, understanding the context and shaping how an experience works. Art Direction gives the work a point of view. Motion & 3D make behaviour, space and feeling tangible. Creative Engineering and Parametric Systems let me test ideas through code, tools and working systems.

AI runs through this as a creative material and a practical capability.

I use these disciplines as one connected practice. A technical experiment can change the visual language; motion can make an interaction easier to understand; a piece of copy can turn a feature into an invitation.
