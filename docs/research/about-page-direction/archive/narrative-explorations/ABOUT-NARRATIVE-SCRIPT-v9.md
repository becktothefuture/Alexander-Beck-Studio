**Editorial text**

My work spans Product Design, Experience Design, Art Direction, Motion & 3D, Creative Engineering and Parametric Systems.

Product and Experience Design shape how something works. Art Direction gives it character. Motion and 3D make behaviour and space tangible. Creative Engineering and Parametric Systems let me explore through code, tools and working systems. AI extends that practice as both a creative material and a practical capability.

The value is in letting these disciplines influence one another: a technical experiment can change the visual language; motion can make an interaction easier to understand.
