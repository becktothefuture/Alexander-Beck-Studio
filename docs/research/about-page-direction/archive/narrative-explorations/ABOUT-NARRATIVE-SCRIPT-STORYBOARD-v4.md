# About Me narrative script and storyboard v4

## Status

**Working narrative for refinement.** V4 keeps the two vertically scrolling editorial areas and the existing modular visual language, while rebuilding the page around one continuous dramatic arc.

Client names, education details, professional positioning and location remain subject to final public verification.

## The objective

The page should introduce Alexander as a person and as a multidisciplinary practitioner. It should explain what he believes, how his background shaped the practice, which capabilities he brings to the work and how they combine in service of ambitious teams.

The visual journey demonstrates that thinking. Complexity begins as a field of unresolved possibility. The disciplines reveal ways of working within it. Their interaction gives the field coherence, movement and character. The bust then provides a quiet human resolution and a clear route to contact.

## Dramatic arc

**An idea without a clear shape → the curiosity to understand it → a practice built across disciplines → the disciplines begin influencing one another → the idea becomes tangible → the person behind the work**

The important change from V3 is that the words in space are no longer atmospheric captions. Every line completes the thought before it and creates a reason for the next visual transformation.

## Complete working script

### 01 — Promise

> I help shape complex ideas into emotionally compelling experiences.

**Visual annotation:** A distant cloud sits ahead in a large, calm space. The opening line is still and easy to read.

### 02 — Entering complexity

> At the beginning, an idea can feel like everything at once.

> It might need to be useful, original, technically possible and emotionally right—all before its shape is clear.

> This is the part I enjoy: learning what is there, understanding what matters to the people involved, and finding the direction that can hold it together.

**Visual annotation:** The three thoughts appear in sequence as the camera approaches and enters the cloud. The material becomes denser as the second line names the competing demands. A calm opening becomes visible as the final line introduces direction.

### Editorial area 1 — Curiosity connects the work

> I have always had one foot in the technical and one in the visual. I studied Computer Science before Communication Design, then built a practice across digital products, identity, interfaces, motion and creative technology. The common thread is curiosity: how something works, how it feels, and how those qualities can strengthen one another.

**Things I keep coming back to**

- Emerging technology with a useful role in people’s lives.
- Products and systems with character.
- Strong visual ideas that also work.
- Making ideas tangible early, because a deck can only do so much.

> That curiosity has shaped work with Yoti, S&P Global, Bentley, SunExpress, McCann and the American Heart Association across identity, finance, mobility, healthcare and digital products.

**Visual annotation:** The cloud opens into a broad, quiet field. This is the first vertical editorial pause: one introductory paragraph, one compact list and one client line.

### 03 — The practice comes into view

> Over time, those interests became a practice built across six connected disciplines.

**Visual annotation:** The camera rises. The calm field resolves into an ordered dot grid, viewed increasingly from above.

### 04 — Six connected disciplines

- Product Design
- Experience Design
- Art Direction
- Motion & 3D
- Creative Engineering
- Parametric Systems

**Visual annotation:** Exactly six positions illuminate within the grid. The labels belong to the highlighted points; they are not independent statements floating through space.

### Editorial area 2 — How the disciplines work together

> Product Design and Experience Design help me understand the opportunity and shape how the experience works. Art Direction gives the idea character and a point of view. Motion & 3D make behaviour, space and feeling tangible. Creative Engineering and Parametric Systems let me explore directly through code, tools and working systems.
>
> AI runs through this practice as both a creative material and a practical capability, expanding what I can explore, generate and build. Judgement, craft and context give those possibilities purpose.
>
> Moving between the disciplines lets one kind of thinking change another. An insight can reshape the product. A technical experiment can unlock the visual language. Motion can make an interaction easier to understand. Each move informs the next until the work begins to feel coherent.

**Visual annotation:** The reader moves vertically through the grid while the relevant points respond to each passage. In the final paragraph, influence begins to travel between them. The six highlights remain legible, but the grid starts behaving as one system.

### 05 — Bringing the idea to life

> As these ways of thinking begin to work together, the idea gathers momentum.

> What was difficult to picture takes on form, behaviour and character.

> It becomes something people can understand, interact with and feel.

> That is what I mean by bringing an idea to life.

**Visual annotation:** The whole grid develops broad waves. The camera drops from the overhead view and returns to forward motion, travelling close above the field. The environment grows more immersive with each sentence, but it remains one transformed system rather than a collection of unrelated objects.

### 06 — Resolution

> This is the role I play for ambitious teams: bringing design, creative technology and AI together to shape new products, services and experiences from early direction to the details that make them convincing.

**Visual annotation:** The field reaches its fullest, most coherent state. The movement then slows. Its particles gather toward a central form, creating a gradual bridge into the bust.

### 07 — Epilogue

> Alexander Beck
>
> Creative technologist, multidisciplinary designer and AI specialist.
>
> Using creativity and technology to shape something new.

Actions: **Email** · **LinkedIn**

**Visual annotation:** The point-cloud bust resolves at a much larger scale than in the current version and revolves slowly. This is a holding moment for the final statement and contact actions, not another narrative chapter.

## Editorial rhythm

There are only two long-form vertical reading areas:

1. **Curiosity connects the work** — background, recurring interests and selected clients.
2. **How the disciplines work together** — the six capabilities, AI and multidisciplinary synthesis.

Everything else is a short connecting thought with a clear narrative job. The spatial copy should remain in sequence; removing or rearranging an individual line should break the logic, not merely change the mood.
