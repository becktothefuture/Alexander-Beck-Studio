# About Me narrative script v6

I help shape complex ideas into emotionally compelling experiences.

At the start, an idea can feel like everything at once. It needs to be useful, original, technically possible and emotionally right—before its shape is clear.

I like working at that point: learning what is there, understanding what matters to the people involved, and finding the organising thought that gives it direction.

That has become the thread through my own practice. I have always been interested in the space where aesthetics and technology meet. I studied Communication Design, then built a practice across digital products, web design, identity, interfaces, motion, and creative uses of AI and technology.

Curiosity connects it all: how things work, how they feel, and how those qualities can strengthen one another.

Emerging technology interests me most when it has a useful role to play in people’s lives. For that to happen, it often needs to be deconstructed, made tangible, made visual and made interactive enough to resonate.

That curiosity has shaped my work with technology start-ups and creative agencies, on ambitious projects across identity, finance, mobility, healthcare and digital products.

[Client logos]

Over time, those interests have grown into a practice built across six connected disciplines: Product Design, Experience Design, Art Direction, Motion & 3D, Creative Engineering and Parametric Systems.

Together, they give me different ways into the same problem. Product Design and Experience Design help me understand an opportunity, explore different ways of thinking, and shape how an experience works. Art Direction gives an idea character and a point of view. Motion & 3D make behaviour, space and feeling tangible. Creative Engineering and Parametric Systems let me explore directly through code, tools and working systems.

AI sits within this practice as both a creative material and a practical capability, expanding what I can explore, generate and build. Moving between disciplines lets one kind of thinking inform another. An insight can reshape a product. A technical experiment can unlock a visual language. Motion can make an interaction easier to understand.

As those ways of thinking begin to influence one another, complexity takes on clear form, intuitive behaviour and a character people can trust. It becomes something they can relate to, interact with and feel.

That is what excites me about bringing an idea to life.

That is the work I do for ambitious teams: bringing design, creative technology and AI together to shape new products, services and experiences—from the earliest direction to the details that make them convincing.

Using creativity and technology to shape something new.
