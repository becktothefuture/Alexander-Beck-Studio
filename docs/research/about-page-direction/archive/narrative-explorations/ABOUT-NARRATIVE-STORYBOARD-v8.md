# About Me storyboard v8

## Status

**Current storyboard direction.** This maps `ABOUT-NARRATIVE-SCRIPT-v8.md` to the current About Narrative worlds and the sectionless global-track model being introduced in the lab. It is a copy and visual-allocation brief, not an implementation change.

The visitor should experience one continuous introduction rather than eight named chapters. World starts set the visual pace; title fields, reading blocks, the discipline reveal and the final interaction sit independently on the global timeline.

No display copy is introduced here. Every line below is from V8. Client context remains a logo block only.

## Story motion

**A complex project appears → unfamiliar territory asks to become intuitive → an aesthetic and technical lens gives it form → six disciplines widen the ways of making → the whole field begins to agree → a direct invitation.**

The point material remains one continuous thing. It moves from cloud to turbulent field, calm field, top-down grid, living field and bust without acquiring separate decorative worlds. The changes describe the work becoming tangible.

## Global track allocation

### World start — `cluster`

**Text field: spatial title**

> I help shape complex ideas into emotionally compelling experiences.

**Visual direction**

The opening remains spare: a distant cluster with space to breathe, then the promise. It should suggest there is more material ahead without turning complexity into a threat or a metaphor that needs explaining.

### World start — `turbulent-field`

**Text field: spatial title**

> Complex projects hold my attention—especially when something new has to become intuitive.

**Text field: spatial title**

> A new technology, an unfamiliar interaction or a question of trust calls for a solution people can understand, use and feel.

**Visual direction**

The camera enters the active cloud. This material is energetic and full, but it is not chaos for its own sake: the movement supports the thought that an unfamiliar proposition must become intuitive. Keep the two title fields as the whole opening run; do not add a third large thought before the first reading pause.

### World start — `calm-field`

**Text field: scroll block**

> I work at the intersection of aesthetics and technology. It is where technology becomes personal, and where early ideas begin to take shape.

> I studied Communication Design to understand how ideas are communicated through composition, form and shape, as much as through words. It taught me how hierarchy and rhythm can change meaning. Since then, I have built work across digital products, web design, identity, interfaces, motion, and creative uses of AI and technology.

> I am especially interested in emerging technology when it can play a useful part in people’s lives. Bringing it closer often means making it tangible, visual and interactive enough to resonate.

> I have worked with technology start-ups and creative agencies on ambitious projects across identity, finance, mobility, healthcare and digital products.

> [Client logos]

**Visual direction**

The turbulent material settles into a broad, calm field and the reading measure becomes stable. This is the first deliberate pause. The writing establishes the professional lens, education and breadth of work without becoming a biography or a client list.

### Continuing `calm-field` — practical bridge and discipline reveal

**Text field: spatial title**

> Different questions call for different ways of making.

**Text/simulation field: world-linked labels**

1. Product Design
2. Experience Design
3. Art Direction

**Text field: scroll block**

> My work spans six connected disciplines: Product Design, Experience Design, Art Direction, Motion & 3D, Creative Engineering and Parametric Systems.

> Product Design and Experience Design help me understand what needs to happen and shape how an experience works. Art Direction gives it character and a point of view. Motion & 3D make behaviour, space and feeling tangible. Creative Engineering and Parametric Systems let me explore through code, tools and working systems.

> AI runs through the work as a creative material and a practical capability. It gives me another way to explore, generate and build.

> These disciplines overlap in the work. An insight can reshape a product. A technical experiment can unlock a visual language. Motion can make an interaction easier to understand.

**Visual direction**

The calm field pitches into the existing high, grid-like view without breaking the camera’s forward journey. Six individual points gain their labels as a single world-linked annotation clip—not six competing title moments. The grid continues past the labels into the reading block. As the final paragraph reaches the screen, local connections and small waves begin to travel through the field: the disciplines are affecting the same material.

### World start — `living-field`

**Text field: spatial title**

**Text field: spatial title**

The whole grid becomes the living field. The new energy comes from the material itself: broad waves, depth, movement and shifting perspective—not added objects, scenery or visual maximalism. The two spatial thoughts articulate what has changed; the service statement arrives in a final calm reading beat. It is the clearest description of what Alexander offers, and it lands before the bust rather than competing with it.

## Allocation guardrails

- Use no additional title copy beyond V8. The opening’s two complexity thoughts are the maximum uninterrupted spatial-title run.
- Let the first scroll block carry the personal and professional background; it should be the first full reading pause after the opener.
- Keep client proof as logos only. Do not restore client names to prose.
- Keep all six literal discipline names in the world-linked reveal and the supporting reading block.
- Treat the living field as the visual proof of practices influencing one another. Do not introduce unrelated objects or symbolic set dressing.
- Keep the service proposition as the last reading block, then let the bust carry only the invitation and CTAs.
- Use World starts as anchors only. Titles, scroll blocks, labels and the interaction clip must remain independently placeable on the global timeline.
