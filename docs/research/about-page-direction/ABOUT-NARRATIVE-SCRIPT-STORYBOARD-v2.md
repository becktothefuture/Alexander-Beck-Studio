# About Me narrative script and storyboard v2

## Status

**Working creative direction for review.** This is a confident script and visual storyboard proposal, not approved public copy or a production implementation brief.

The script uses confirmed themes from the interviews and source matrix. It deliberately avoids project-specific role, outcome and client claims that still require verification.

## Narrative destination

The page should demonstrate how Alexander works rather than merely describe a multidisciplinary profile.

**Promise → complexity → method → ingredients → synthesis → signature**

The point-cloud world is one continuous material. The reader enters complexity, emerges into enough calm to understand the method, sees six disciplines become active ingredients, then watches them combine into one living experience. The bust is a quiet epilogue for the final invitation, not the climax.

## Voice

- Direct and information-first.
- Human without conversational filler.
- Precise enough to picture the action.
- Confident without superlatives or claims of mastery.
- One idea per sentence wherever possible.
- The visuals carry atmosphere; the copy carries meaning.

## Complete working script

### 01 — Promise

> I help ambitious teams shape complex ideas into clear, human and emotionally compelling experiences.

### 02 — Complexity

> The most interesting problems contain more than can fit in a brief.
>
> People, systems, constraints and competing possibilities pull in different directions.
>
> I do not start by simplifying them. I start by getting close enough to understand what is really there.

### 03 — Background and method

> I came to design through language and technology, then visual form. That path grew into products, interactions and systems. The scale changed. The way I work became clearer.

**1. Get close**
Learn the people, context and constraints.

**2. Find the organising idea**
Decide what matters and what connects.

**3. Make it tangible**
Build a prototype people can see, question and improve together.

### 04 — Ingredients

> The work can call for different disciplines.

- Product Design
- Experience Design
- Art Direction
- Motion & 3D
- Creative Engineering
- Parametric Systems

> Each offers a different way to clarify, test or express the same idea.

### 05 — Synthesis and ambition

> These are ingredients, not separate answers.
>
> A clear point of view helps them work together, so the idea stays intact from the first prototype to the final detail. That is when the work comes alive.
>
> I want more problems too complex for one discipline, and too human for a formula.

### Epilogue — Signature

> Bring me something unresolved.

Primary action: **Start a conversation**
Secondary action: **LinkedIn**

## Canonical image-generation contract

All three generated sheets must use this fixed contract.

### Canvas and panel system

- One landscape 16:9 storyboard sheet.
- Exactly four equal panels in a strict 2×2 grid.
- Read left-to-right across the first row, then left-to-right across the second row.
- Consistent outer margin and equal gutters.
- No montage overlap, tilted panels or decorative scrapbook treatment.
- Every panel shows the same complete desktop website shell at the same scale.

### Site shell

- Use the supplied current About screenshot as the authoritative shell reference.
- Preserve the pale neutral wall, physical inset frame, large rounded studio window, subtle grain, quiet footer and persistent bottom Button Bar.
- Keep the About Me tab selected with the authored blue accent.
- The shell never animates or changes geometry between panels.
- No browser chrome, laptop mockup, device frame or floating presentation card.

### Visual material

- All spatial imagery is built from solid flat circular dots.
- Use scale, density, position, displacement and motion to communicate depth.
- Neutrals dominate. Use deep green, cobalt blue, orange and acid yellow-green only as controlled signal material.
- Dots may form a flat lattice, broad terrain, solid dot-built monoliths or a sparse airborne formation.
- No thin connector lines, helper rings, wireframes, trails, transparent overlapping circles, glow, lens flare, fog, lighting gradients or photoreal objects.
- No shaded spheres. No 3D lighting. No cast shadows on dots.
- The climax may be visually dense, but every element must visibly grow from the six expertise seeds.

### Typography and text

- Opening route-entry text uses a refined high-contrast editorial serif similar in role to Instrument Serif.
- All subsequent narrative copy, discipline labels and actions use a clean contemporary sans similar in role to Geist.
- Operational micro-labels may use a restrained mono face.
- Render supplied text verbatim, once only, with correct spelling and punctuation.
- Do not paraphrase, abbreviate, add headings, add lorem ipsum or invent interface copy.
- Preserve a clear reading plane. Dots may pass around text but never make it illegible.

### Quality and avoidance

- Production-grade product-design storyboard, not loose concept art.
- Clean editorial hierarchy, restrained physical texture and plausible WebGL rendering.
- No watermarks, logos, awards, client names, portfolio cards, decorative artefacts or extra controls.
- Do not reproduce the large all-caps copy from the older concept references.
- Do not turn the flat-dot language into stars, dust, smoke or generic particles.

## Review criteria

Keep a generated sheet only if it passes all of these checks:

1. The persistent shell remains visually stable across its four panels.
2. The panel sequence is unambiguous.
3. All required narrative text is real, readable and correctly spelled.
4. The dots remain solid, flat and recognizably part of the site material language.
5. Every visual change advances the narrative rather than adding atmosphere alone.
6. The expertise dots remain part of the field and become the cause of the culmination.
7. The climax demonstrates integration rather than simply increasing object count.
8. The bust reads as a calm, larger epilogue with clear CTA space.
