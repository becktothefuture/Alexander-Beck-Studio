## 04 - Editorial text

Communication Design gave me a way to study how meaning is made: through words, images, hierarchy, motion, systems and the spaces between them.

Agency work sharpened the visual side: culture, pace and craft. Product work made the questions more practical: how a service explains itself, how a screen earns trust, how a system holds together when people actually use it.

Emerging technology keeps my attention because it asks for that same translation. A capability is not the same as an experience. It becomes useful when people can see it, try it, question it and understand where it fits.

My experience has taken me through startups and creative agencies, across digital products, web design, identity, interfaces, motion and creative technology, with projects in finance, mobility, healthcare, digital identity and product innovation.

## 08 - Editorial text

Product and Experience Design help me understand the opportunity, users and behaviour of an experience. Art Direction gives the idea a recognisable point of view. Motion & 3D make timing, space and response easier to feel. Creative Engineering and Parametric Systems let me test ideas through code, tools and working systems.

AI belongs in this mix as a working material. It helps me explore options, build tools around the task and push a direction further without handing over judgement.

The value is in letting these disciplines affect one another. A technical experiment can change the visual language. A motion study can make an interaction easier to understand. A line of copy can make a feature feel obvious instead of explained.

## 12 - Editorial text

That is what I mean by bringing an idea to life: finding the structure it can stand on, the behaviour people can trust and the character that makes it feel specific.

It can happen through an interface, a motion system, a prototype, an AI workflow or a small tool that changes the conversation. The format changes with the problem. The aim stays consistent: make the idea visible enough for better decisions, and real enough for people to respond to.

I help ambitious teams bring design, creative technology and AI together from early direction to the details that make an experience convincing.
