## 04 - Editorial text

My way into this started with language. I was interested in how a thought changes when it becomes a sentence, a composition, an interface or a system.

Communication Design gave me a practical way to study that: hierarchy, rhythm, form and attention. Agency work gave me a grounding in visual culture and craft. Digital products then moved the same questions into behaviour, trust and use.

That movement suited me. I like the point where aesthetics meet mechanics, where something has to look clear and also behave clearly.

For technology to earn a place in people's lives, it has to be more than impressive. It has to be legible, useful and trustworthy enough for people to understand what it is doing.

Much of my career has formed around that challenge, with technology startups and creative agencies across identity, finance, mobility, healthcare and digital products.

## 08 - Editorial text

The six disciplines are not a menu I move through in order. Product Design and Experience Design help me find the opportunity and define the behaviour. Art Direction gives the idea a voice. Motion & 3D make response, space and feeling visible. Creative Engineering and Parametric Systems help me build the idea far enough to learn from it.

AI sits inside that process as a material for exploration and a capability for making. It opens more routes through a problem, but it still needs direction.

The useful moments often happen between disciplines. Code suggests a form. Motion clarifies a flow. A brand decision changes how a product explains itself.
