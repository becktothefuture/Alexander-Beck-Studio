# Homepage title composition — integrated stamp study

## Shared composition brief

- Preserve the existing homepage, frame, navigation, copy, and coloured-ball simulation.
- Render the exact wording: “Alexander Beck” and “Designer & Technologist”.
- Treat the complete title as one built typographic object, not a large signature with a small caption.
- Use three or four tightly related lines. No line may feel more than about 20% larger or heavier than another.
- Keep one coherent stroke-weight system throughout the name and role.
- Balance line lengths through line breaks, spacing, and restrained extensions.
- Use compact, even leading and form a clear rectangle, oval, lozenge, or seal-like silhouette.
- Keep the lockup confidently modest in scale within the homepage rather than dominating the canvas.
- Use warm near-black ink. Avoid romantic wedding calligraphy, oversized initials, cartoon nostalgia, giant swashes, and childlike handwriting.

## 01 — Warm soft-serif block

**A — Four-line rectangle**
Set “Alexander / Beck / Designer & / Technologist” as a centred compact block in a warm, softly sculpted editorial serif. Use consistent dark stroke mass, tight optical spacing, modest old-style character, and restrained terminals. Make the four lines read as one rectangular stamp.

**B — Three-line lozenge**
Set “Alexander Beck / Designer & / Technologist” as a compact three-line soft-serif insignia. Keep the name only slightly wider than the role lines and shape the outer contour into a balanced horizontal lozenge.

## 02 — Retro italic insignia

Set “Alexander / Beck / Designer & / Technologist” in a mature rounded 1970s editorial italic. Maintain comparable cap height and stroke mass on every line. Use only small terminal extensions to align the stepped line endings into one forward-moving stamp.

**B — Three-line oval**
Set “Alexander Beck / Designer & / Technologist” in the same restrained retro italic system. Compact the spacing and align the entry and exit terminals so the whole lockup forms one quiet oval emblem, without decorative flourishes.

## 03 — Monoline Schriftbild

**A — Four-line seal**
Build “Alexander / Beck / Designer & / Technologist” as a four-line monoline script seal. Use one firm, slightly imperfect pen stroke throughout, open counters, and short structural line extensions that square the composition. It must feel authored by a creative director, not romantic or casual.

**B — Three-line interlock**
Build “Alexander Beck / Designer & / Technologist” as a three-line monoline Schriftbild. Interlock ascenders, descenders, and word endings only where they help the lines share one oval silhouette. Avoid large loops and signature-style gestures.
