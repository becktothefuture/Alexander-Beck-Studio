## What to build

Apply a restrained wide-and-tall desktop density refinement to the About editorial and Discipline material, then certify the complete responsive About experience and shared viewport cover. The adjustment must make large displays feel intentionally composed without changing the standard desktop reference or the stable outer shell.

## Acceptance criteria

- The large 1920 × 1080 reference uses space more confidently while preserving readable measures and hierarchy.
- The standard 1440 × 1000 desktop reference remains visually stable.
- No wide-screen rule affects short viewports that should show the cover.
- The outer wall, physical frame, Button Bar, theme, cursor, and route-entry typography contracts remain unchanged.
- The full About Sequence is captured at large desktop, standard desktop, laptop, tablet, and two portrait-phone references.
- Unsupported phone-landscape, short-desktop, extreme-wide, and extreme-tall cases have explicit cover captures.
- Cover behavior is verified on Home, Work, About Me, Lab, and Contact.
- Final browser certification passes in Chromium and WebKit.
- The canonical site gate passes.
- Generated contact sheets and a concise report identify the checked routes, browsers, viewport sizes, and Story WU samples.
- The final diff contains no unrelated edits and preserves pre-existing working-tree changes.
