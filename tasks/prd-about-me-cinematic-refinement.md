The camera stops with the scroll and holds at the page end. The material continues.

The final surface must occupy the entire usable width without showing its outer bounds. This supersedes the earlier finite-island and visible-perimeter composition. Keep the geometry bounded in Blender for implementation, but place its authored edges outside the visible frustum or beyond the existing distance fog. Do not shrink the world to fit a complete object inside the frame.

**Reading and camera correction from Alex, 31 August:** use the full viewport for text, not an upper reading band. Remove forced upward offsets from narrative titles. Keep one constant physical camera distance per native scroll distance, in both directions; no separate camera settling, mouse drift, section-speed schedule or ending brake. Test the stronger direction in one small moving study. If it is only an ornamental wave, revise that study before integrating it; do not compensate with more prose or effects.

## 7. Invitation and perpetual motion

The source currently derives action opacity from 62–80% progress through the invitation and keeps actions inert below 0.98 opacity. A readable title can therefore precede a usable invitation. Replace that dependency with an About-local arrival state.

- Enter once the invitation is reached, without requiring an early camera lock. Complete title, rule, support and both actions on elapsed time without more scroll, within 1.2 seconds including any shared title pause. Aim for 900ms; do not change the shared entrance of other routes. Ordered starts may overlap within this local budget; do not leave controls inert while waiting for a long decorative fade.
- Direct-end restoration and reduced motion show the complete usable state immediately.
- Reverse out cancels the arrival and removes invisible controls from focus. If a control being hidden owns focus, return it to the About scrollport without scrolling. Do not steal unrelated focus.
- Re-entry starts a clean invitation sequence but does not restart the material's phase. Teardown cancels local state and listeners. Avoid timers or React updates on every frame.
- Drive material movement from the current animation owner with bounded phase. Pause when hidden. Resume continuously without accumulated time jumps; hold scene motion static for reduced motion.
- Provide one accessible About motion pause/resume control using the existing quiet utility styling. There is no pause control in the current `ShellUtilityRail`; do not assume the sound control does this. Add a narrowly scoped control through an explicit utility integration, without introducing a second navigation system or changing other routes' controls. Verify the extra target's mobile spacing before choosing its placement.

Continuing non-essential motion needs user control alongside the content; reduced-motion support alone is not a substitute for an effective pause mechanism. Check the implementation against [W3C Pause, Stop, Hide](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/pause-stop-hide.html). This plan is not an accessibility certification.
